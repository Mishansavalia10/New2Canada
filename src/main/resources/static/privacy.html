Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

What we collect

  • Sign-in details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, name, email address and profile photo through Firebase Authentication. We store them in our database to recognise you.
  • Search history. When you are signed in and the server runs with its database enabled, each search you run is saved with your account so you can see your recent searches and the most common ones.
  • Anonymous use. If you are not signed in, we do not link your searches to you. The server keeps a short, in-memory count of recent queries for the statistics shown on the home page.
  • Listings. We store apartment listings collected from public rental websites: title, address, city, province, rent, bedrooms, source site and the link to the original posting.

How we use it

To run the search, keep your history, and show site statistics. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.
